ENTER Jan

Rui: Jan, how do you measure whether a student has learned something?
Jan: Officially? Test scores. Quarterly assessments, tracked by the administration.
Rui, confused: And unofficially?
Jan, smile: Whether they ask a question I haven't been asked before.
Rui: Does that happen often?
Jan, annoyed: Less than it should. The curriculum is old. It was designed before a quarter of these facilities even existed. I teach a standard atmospheric systems module and half the equipment described in it has been replaced twice.
Rui: Have you proposed updates?
Jan: Twice. Got told the curriculum review committee meets every five years.
Rui, confused: When's the next meeting?
Jan: Three years ago. It keeps getting postponed.
Rui: Does Eugene know?
Jan, smile: Eugene thinks inspiration transcends the curriculum. He's not entirely wrong. But it helps to have accurate materials too.
